Why digital markets concentrate — and on what terms

Trigger Question Why do so many digital markets converge on one or two dominant firms?
FRAMEWORK

Winner-Takes-All Markets

Network effects + multi-homing costs + weak niches → market tips to one firm.

FRAMEWORK

Two-Sided Markets — Market Structure View

Rochet & Tirole — the platform itself is the unit of competition.

FRAMEWORK

Multi-Sided Platforms

Marketplace vs. Platform

All marketplaces are platforms; not all platforms are marketplaces.

FRAMEWORK

GAFAM Concentration

Five firms · disproportionate share of digital market value (~48% of top 100).
